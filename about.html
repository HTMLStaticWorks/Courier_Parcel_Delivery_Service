Our Story

Driven by Speed, Built on Trust

Founded in 2020, SWIFT began with a simple idea: local deliveries shouldn't be complicated or opaque. We noticed a gap in the market for a courier service that combined the speed of local riders with the technology of major logistics networks.

Today, we operate a vast fleet of delivery vehicles and utilize a state-of-the-art tracking system to ensure that whether you are sending a personal gift or fulfilling hundreds of e-commerce orders, your parcels are in the safest hands.

Our Mission

To provide seamless, reliable, and affordable courier services that connect people and businesses across our delivery zones. We aim to take the stress out of logistics through transparency and technology.

Our Vision

To become the region's most trusted logistics partner, setting the standard for speed, sustainability, and customer-centric delivery experiences.

How We Calculate Rates

Final pricing may depend on the following factors:

Parcel Size & Weight

Rates are categorized into Small, Medium, Large, and Extra Large based on dimensions and weight.

Delivery Zones

Distance matters. We separate zones into Local (within city), Nearby (surrounding areas), and Extended.

Delivery Type

Choose between Standard (2-3 days), Express (Next day), or Same-Day delivery based on urgency.
